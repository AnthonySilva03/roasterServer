Flame Assist

Nudges the flame in small steps so the rate of rise tracks a target. You stay in charge: moving the slider pauses it for one interval, and Drop or a safety cutoff switches it off.

Off
Assist acts only while recording, after Charge, and when a rate of rise is available.
How assist works
Every interval it compares the smoothed RoR with the target. Below the target minus the dead band it raises the flame by one step; above it lowers it. It never moves outside the min/max bounds. Gas burners respond slowly, so keep the interval at 20 s or more and the step small. "Follow the reference" uses the reference roast's RoR at the same elapsed time.

Alarms

Each alarm fires once per roast when its condition is met. Alarms are saved on this device.

How alarms work
Conditions are checked on every sensor sample. "Active from" delays the alarm until that event is marked, so a 190 °C alarm from First Crack will not fire during drying. "Set flame" moves the servo and logs the change as a roast event. Speech uses the browser's voice, so it works offline.
